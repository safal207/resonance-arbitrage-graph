# Async Design-Partner Questionnaire

Use after a prospect replies to the burning question. Keep it short and answerable by email.

1. What kind of proposed trades does the agent produce today?
2. What must be true before a proposed trade is allowed to reach the executor?
3. Which failure is hardest to detect before submission: stale price, slippage, insufficient depth, latency, permissions, settlement assumptions, or something else?
4. What evidence do you retain after the decision, and can you replay why the trade was allowed or blocked?
5. What is the consequence when the control fails: lost edge, loss, manual review, user trust, compliance burden, or downtime?
6. Where should an independent verifier fit: CLI, SDK, API, MCP tool, CI/replay report, or dashboard?
7. Could you share one public, fixture, or sandbox example without credentials or sensitive architecture?
8. Would a 14-day paper-only pilot that returns a false-opportunity map, benchmark, and integration recommendation be useful?
9. Who owns this decision boundary internally?
10. What would need to be true for this to become a paid tool rather than an interesting report?
