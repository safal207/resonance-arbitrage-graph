## Calibration batch

Start with five individually researched contacts. Do not expand until their replies are coded into recurring pains and the burning question is revised if needed.

## Cadence

- one initial email;
- one follow-up after three business days if there is no reply;
- then stop;
- email-only, async;
- no sensitive architecture, credentials, production probing, or performance promises.
