# Opportunity Truth Founding List — Landing Copy

## Hero

### Before your agent trades, verify the opportunity.

RESONANCE Verify checks whether a proposed crypto trade still survives executable depth, quote freshness, modeled fees, slippage, market regime, policy, and evidence requirements before it reaches an existing executor.

**CTA:** Join the Opportunity Truth Founding List

## Who this is for

Builders of autonomous or semi-autonomous trading systems who already have:

- a strategy or signal source;
- an exchange or execution connection;
- risk limits or account permissions;
- paper or sandbox workflows;

but still need an independent answer to:

> Why was this exact proposed trade allowed, observed, or rejected?

## What founding members receive

- the first evidence-bound Opportunity Funnel case studies;
- publication-safe Opportunity Truth Benchmark updates;
- invitations to submit public, fixture, or sandbox examples;
- early access to the paper-only design-partner pilot;
- influence over the first integration surface: CLI, SDK, API, MCP, or replay report.

## Suggested form fields

Required:

- work email;
- company/project;
- role;
- what kind of trades the agent proposes;
- hardest pre-trade failure to catch.

Optional:

- preferred integration: CLI / SDK / API / MCP / report;
- public or sandbox example available: yes / no;
- interested in a 14-day paper-only pilot: yes / no.

Never request:

- exchange credentials;
- wallet keys;
- production secrets;
- account balances;
- signing or transfer permissions.
