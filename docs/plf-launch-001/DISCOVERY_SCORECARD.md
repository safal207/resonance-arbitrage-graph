## Buying-signal ladder

```text
B0 curiosity
B1 real pain described
B2 public/sandbox sample offered
B3 pilot or integration interest
B4 budget / procurement / decision-owner signal
B5 paid pilot
```

## Expansion gate

Do not expand the first five-contact calibration batch until replies have been coded.

After calibration, continue toward 20 targeted emails only when:

- the burning question still elicits useful operational answers; and
- personalization remains based on one verified public product/control claim.

## Product decision rule

Prioritize a capability only after either:

- three independent prospects request the same capability; or
- one paid design partner requests it and the requirement clearly generalizes.

Generic praise, stars, impressions, and repository traffic do not count as buying evidence.
