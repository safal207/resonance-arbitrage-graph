# Discovery Signal Coding

After each reply, assign one primary pain and one buying signal.

## Pain codes

- `STALE_EVIDENCE`
- `SLIPPAGE_OR_DEPTH`
- `LATENCY_OR_EDGE_DECAY`
- `POLICY_OR_PERMISSION`
- `EXPLAINABILITY_OR_AUDIT`
- `PAPER_QUALIFICATION`
- `REPLAY_OR_INCIDENT_REVIEW`
- `MULTI_VENUE_STATE`
- `OTHER`

## Buying-signal levels

- `B0_CURIOSITY` — says the topic is interesting.
- `B1_PAIN_CONFIRMED` — describes a real workflow/failure.
- `B2_SAMPLE_OFFERED` — will share a public/sandbox case.
- `B3_PILOT_INTEREST` — wants a bounded pilot or asks about integration.
- `B4_BUDGET_SIGNAL` — discusses price, procurement, or decision owner.
- `B5_PAID_PILOT` — written agreement and payment path.

## Feature decision rule

Do not prioritize a product feature from one reply. Require either:

- three independent prospects requesting the same capability; or
- one paid design partner plus evidence that the capability generalizes beyond its private implementation.
