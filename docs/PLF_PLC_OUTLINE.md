# RESONANCE Verify — PLF Content Outline

## Pre-prelaunch question

When your trading agent finds a trade that looks profitable, what evidence must survive before it may submit the order—and which failure is hardest to catch today: stale price, slippage, thin liquidity, policy breach, or an unexplained decision?

## PLC1 — The false-opportunity problem

Promise: understand why a visible spread is not yet an executable trade.

Teach:
- top-of-book price is not executable depth;
- fees and slippage are route-specific;
- quote freshness and latency can erase edge;
- cross-venue profit claims hide inventory and settlement state;
- a trading agent needs a pre-trade evidence boundary, not only an account limit.

CTA: join the Opportunity Truth list and share one public/sandbox example.

## PLC2 — From signal to verified decision

Show a clearly labeled paper-only case:

`candidate -> costs -> liquidity -> freshness -> regime -> base verdict -> final verdict -> later outcome -> evidence`

Teach the difference between:
- a signal;
- a valid proposed action;
- an executable route;
- a later paper outcome;
- a live-fill claim.

CTA: request an async Opportunity Truth audit.

## PLC3 — Install the gate without replacing your stack

Show:

`existing strategy -> RESONANCE Verify -> ALLOW/OBSERVE/REJECT -> existing executor`

The design partner keeps strategy IP, custody, venue accounts, and execution infrastructure. RESONANCE verifies the proposed action and returns evidence.

CTA: apply for the founding paper-only pilot.

## Open cart

Three founding design-partner slots.

- 14 days;
- one strategy;
- one venue;
- public/sandbox data;
- async email and shared evidence only;
- no keys, orders, signing, transfers, or live funds;
- benchmark, false-opportunity map, evidence samples, and integration recommendation.

Honest scarcity: manual onboarding and evidence review capacity, not artificial SaaS scarcity.

Restate who the pilot is for, the paper-only boundary, the deliverables, and the exact deadline. Do not imply guaranteed performance or urgency based on market returns.
