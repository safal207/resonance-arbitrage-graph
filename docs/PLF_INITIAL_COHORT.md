## Personalization rule

Each email must reference one public control or product claim and ask how the team verifies a proposed trade before submission. Do not send a generic platform pitch.

## Core question

When your agent finds a trade that looks profitable, what evidence must survive before it may submit the order—and which failure is hardest to catch today: stale price, slippage, thin liquidity, policy breach, or an unexplained decision?

## First email structure

- one sentence showing the specific product/control observed;
- the burning question;
- one sentence on RESONANCE Verify as a paper-only pre-trade verification layer;
- permissionless offer to analyze one public/sandbox example asynchronously;
- no request for secrets or production architecture.
