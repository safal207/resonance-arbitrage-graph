# v0.12 — Stability Decomposition

v0.12 explains temporal instability found by v0.11 without feeding post-hoc information back into policy selection.

```text
verified replay bundle
  -> verified v0.11 walk-forward report
  -> fold selected policy (already frozen by calibration)
  -> recompute calibration diagnostics under that policy
  -> recompute untouched validation diagnostics under the same policy
  -> compare market/state/outcome evidence
  -> diagnostic instability drivers
  -> deterministic decomposition report
```

The decomposition layer is **post-hoc and advisory**. A reported driver is an evidence-backed diagnostic association, not proof that the driver physically caused the validation failure.

## Drivers

Per failed fold the report can surface:

- `REGIME_DRIFT` — total-variation distance between derived regime distributions crosses the configured threshold.
- `ROUTE_DRIFT` — total-variation distance between semantic route distributions crosses the configured threshold.
- `LIQUIDITY_DETERIORATION` — median route-bound top-of-book capacity ratio deteriorates by more than the configured fraction.
- `FRESHNESS_DETERIORATION` — median route-bound quote age increases by more than the configured number of milliseconds.
- `PREDICTION_BIAS_WORSENING` — negative prediction error / overprediction penalty worsens out of sample.
- `CAUSAL_SUPPORT_LOSS` — execute or volatility final-verdict support rate from the nested v0.10 2x2 evidence falls out of sample.
- `UNEXPLAINED_FAILURE` — the fold failed but none of the configured drift thresholds fired.
- `INSUFFICIENT_DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE` — the failed fold has no selected policy or too little diagnostic population.

## No-lookahead contract

v0.12 first calls the v0.11 full bundle-binding verifier. Therefore fold membership, outcome-availability boundaries, candidate selection and nested v0.10 reports must reproduce from the exact replay bundle before decomposition starts.

The selected policy for a fold is never changed by v0.12. Calibration and validation are replayed under the **same already-selected pair**:

```text
execute_net_edge_bps
volatile_return_bps
```

Outcomes are used only after the fold has completed, for diagnostic prediction-bias comparison. They never participate in candidate selection.

## Metrics

For every diagnosable fold:

```text
regime_tv_distance
route_tv_distance
calibration_median_capacity_ratio
validation_median_capacity_ratio
capacity_ratio_drop_fraction
calibration_median_quote_age_ms
validation_median_quote_age_ms
quote_age_increase_ms
calibration_overprediction_penalty_bps
validation_overprediction_penalty_bps
overprediction_penalty_increase_bps
calibration_execute_support_rate
validation_execute_support_rate
calibration_volatility_support_rate
validation_volatility_support_rate
causal_support_rate_drop
```

Drift thresholds are explicit caller inputs. They are diagnostic guardrails, not universal market constants or trading recommendations.

Failed folds remain in the denominator even when they cannot be explained. Aggregate output reports failed-fold driver coverage, per-driver counts and a deterministic primary driver.

## Evidence

Schema:

```text
resonance.arbitrage.stability-decomposition-report/v0.1
```

The envelope binds the replay bundle SHA-256, v0.11 report SHA-256, decomposition thresholds, per-fold selected policy, drift metrics, observed and attributed drivers, aggregate driver counts and the explicit post-hoc/advisory semantic flags.

The lightweight envelope verifier checks canonical structure, digest and driver/metric consistency. The full bundle-binding verifier reruns the entire v0.11 report and v0.12 decomposition from the replay bundle and requires exact canonical equality.

## CLI

`resonance-stability-decomposition` reruns v0.11 from a local replay bundle and then emits v0.12 decomposition evidence. It performs no network requests.

Example diagnostic thresholds:

```bash
resonance-stability-decomposition replay-bundle.json \
  --execute-threshold-bps 25,30,35,40 \
  --volatile-threshold-bps 20,40,60,75 \
  --initial-calibration-operations 20 \
  --validation-operations 10 \
  --min-calibration-truth-events 10 \
  --min-validation-truth-events 5 \
  --min-truth-lower-bound 0.60 \
  --min-survival-lower-bound 0.70 \
  --regime-tv-threshold 0.25 \
  --route-tv-threshold 0.25 \
  --capacity-drop-fraction-threshold 0.25 \
  --quote-age-increase-ms-threshold 500 \
  --overprediction-increase-bps-threshold 10 \
  --causal-support-rate-drop-threshold 0.20
```

These values are examples only.

## Safety boundary

v0.12 is offline/paper-only. It adds no private exchange APIs, account access, signing, orders, transfers, wallets, bridges, daemon or live execution.
