# RESONANCE Verify — Product Discovery & PLF

Status: market-validation phase.

This document intentionally freezes new research layers until repeated buyer evidence identifies the next product priority.

## Product thesis

RESONANCE Verify is an independent, paper-only pre-trade verification layer for autonomous financial agents.

`proposed trade -> executable-state checks -> ALLOW/OBSERVE/REJECT -> replayable evidence`

The first wedge is crypto arbitrage because it creates short-horizon, measurable candidate outcomes. The product is not an arbitrage signal seller and does not promise returns.

## Primary ICP

Builders of autonomous or semi-autonomous crypto trading agents who already have signal generation and execution access, but need a stronger qualification, risk, and evidence boundary before order submission.

## Burning question

When your trading agent finds a trade that looks profitable, what evidence must survive before it may submit the order—and which failure is hardest to catch today: stale price, slippage, thin liquidity, policy breach, or an unexplained decision?

## Product Launch Formula sequence

### Pre-prelaunch — listen

- Build a 30-company target list.
- Send five individually researched emails first.
- Ask the burning question; do not pitch a broad platform.
- Record pain, current workaround, urgency, owner, available sandbox/public evidence, and willingness to run a bounded pilot.

Exit gate: at least 8 substantive replies from 20 targeted emails, 5 repeating the same pain, 3 willing to provide a public/sandbox example, and 1 paid-pilot signal.

### PLC1 — opportunity

Theme: `Visible edge is not executable edge.`

Explain where apparently profitable trades disappear: fees, slippage, depth, freshness, latency, regime change, permissions, and settlement assumptions.

CTA: join the Opportunity Truth list and submit one public/sandbox example.

### PLC2 — transformation

Show one fully labeled, non-live case:

`raw signal -> cost model -> market-state checks -> verdict -> later paper outcome -> evidence receipt`

No invented performance metrics. Fixtures remain clearly labeled.

### PLC3 — ownership

Publish the integration pattern:

`agent proposal -> RESONANCE Verify -> ALLOW/OBSERVE/REJECT -> existing execution system`

Explain what the builder keeps: signal model, execution stack, custody, and strategy IP.

### Open cart — founding design partner pilot

Offer three async, paper-only pilot slots because integration and evidence review are manual.

Pilot hypothesis:

- 14 days;
- one strategy and one venue;
- public or sandbox data only;
- no credentials, orders, wallet signing, transfers, or live capital;
- deliverables: false-opportunity map, Opportunity Truth Benchmark, evidence examples, and integration recommendation.

Pricing is tested only after discovery. Working hypothesis: USD 750–1,500 depending on data preparation and integration effort.

## Initial product ladder

1. Free: public Opportunity Truth content and one bounded example.
2. Paid audit: one strategy/workflow, short evidence report.
3. Founding pilot: 14-day paper-only verification campaign.
4. Later, only after repeated demand: Verify API/SDK or continuous benchmark subscription.

## Roadmap decision rules

- Three or more prospects ask for programmatic integration -> build Verify API/SDK.
- Three or more ask for cross-venue execution truth -> model inventory/rebalance/settlement before enabling it.
- Three or more ask for ranking quality and the corpus is sufficient -> evaluate predictive ranking.
- Enterprise teams ask for permission/audit controls -> package authority, lineage, and receipts.
- Retail prospects mainly ask for guaranteed returns or signals -> do not reposition into a profit-promising bot.

## Evidence board

For every conversation capture:

- company and role;
- current agent/trading workflow;
- decision submitted today;
- hardest false-opportunity failure;
- current control/workaround;
- consequence of failure;
- urgency;
- requested integration form;
- public/sandbox example available;
- willingness to pay;
- next action.

## Safety and claim boundary

RESONANCE Verify remains paper-only and public/sandbox-data only during discovery. It is not investment advice, a broker, an exchange, an execution venue, a custody provider, or a guarantee of profitability. Public benchmark claims require a reproducible quality-ready real-market corpus.
